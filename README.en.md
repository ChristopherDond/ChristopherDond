## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Systems Analysis and Development student** (Senac — Taboão da Serra, Brazil), a fullstack developer who builds **complete applications end-to-end** — authentication, databases and deployment — for web, mobile and embedded systems.

I like turning real-world problems into working software: from **Python automation** and **REST APIs** to **mobile apps (Flutter/Kotlin)** and **Arduino embedded projects**.

- 🎓 Studying **Systems Analysis and Development** at Senac (Aug 2025 – Apr 2027)
- 🏗️ Building fullstack apps with **Node.js + PostgreSQL** (back) and **Next.js/TypeScript** (front)
- 📱 Exploring **Flutter (Dart)** and **Kotlin** for mobile
- 🤖 Playing with **Arduino & embedded systems**
